AI bookkeeping via WhatsApp

CaribBooks is an AI bookkeeper that lives in WhatsApp. Business owners message their transactions — in text, voice, or photos — and the system posts double-entry journal entries, maintains a full chart of accounts, tracks vendors and customers, generates 11 financial reports on demand, and handles GCT and TRN compliance automatically. No software to install. No training. No data entry. Available 24/7.

Text to ledger
Message your transaction in plain English or patois. CB posts the double-entry journal entry instantly.
Voice notes & images
Leave a voice note or snap a receipt. CB transcribes, reads, extracts, and posts the entry.
11 financial reports
P&L, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, and more. Request any report in-chat. Delivered as a branded PDF.
