## 1 · SUBJECT

A single length of yellow polyethylene barrier tape, printed in bold black
capitals **PAID IN CASH · DO NOT CROSS**, stretched across a wet petrol station
forecourt at blue hour.

The tape cordons off bare concrete. Nothing lies inside it — no object, no
debris, no marker. Warm canopy lamps burn behind it, an indigo sky sits above,
and long reflections of the lamps run down the wet ground.

The tape fills the width of the frame and is the only sharp thing in the
photograph.

---

## 2 · FRAME

**Camera height:** 1.35 m — below standing eye level. The tape crosses at chest
height and the eye sits under it, so the cordon reads as something you would
have to duck beneath.

**Distance to tape:** 2.4 m. Focus plane locked here.

**Camera tilt:** 2° down. Gives the concrete more frame than the sky and puts the
horizon of the canopy just above centre.

**Lens:** 85 mm prime, 11-blade rounded aperture.

**Aperture:** f/1.8. Depth of field at 2.4 m is approximately 4 cm — the printed
letters are sharp and the tape's own far edge is already softening.

**Shutter:** 1/125 s. Freezes the wind ripple without smearing the edge.

**ISO:** 400.

**Sensor:** full-frame, 45 MP class.

---

## 3 · LIGHT

**Sky — ambient key.** Blue hour, sun 5° below the horizon. Colour temperature
around 11,000 K. Soft, omnidirectional, no visible source. Gradient runs deep
indigo at the top of frame to a warmer slate at the horizon line. It fills the
shadows so nothing crushes to black.

**Canopy LEDs — practical key.** 4000 K panels recessed into the forecourt
canopy soffit, overhead and 4–6 m behind the tape. Hard-edged pools on the
ground with visible falloff between them. They:

- rim the upper edge of the tape with a broken specular line
- pass through the plastic from behind, so the yellow glows and the black print
  reads faintly as a shadow through the material
- throw long vertical streaks down the wet concrete

**Ground bounce.** Wet concrete returns warm light upward onto the underside of
the tape, lifting it out of silhouette.

**Ratio:** roughly 3:1 warm practical to cool ambient. The 7,000 K split between
sky and canopy is the entire colour structure of the frame — warm where light
lands, cold everywhere else.

**No fill, no flash, no light source in frame.**

---

## 4 · THE TAPE

75 mm low-density polyethylene barrier tape, 0.05 mm gauge. Matte on the printed
face, marginally glossier on the reverse.

**Geometry**
- Enters at the left frame edge, exits at the right, unbroken
- Crosses at 56% of frame height
- Tilted 3° downward left to right
- Gentle catenary sag between anchor points — the lowest point sits about 4 cm
  below the straight line between its ends
- One section, roughly a third in from the right, has **twisted through 90°** and
  shows its duller unprinted reverse before flattening out again

**Surface**
- **Stress-whitening** along the twist and at the sag's deepest point — polyethylene
  goes pale and cloudy where it has been stretched
- Fine longitudinal creases from being rolled on the spool, running the full length
- Two or three stress wrinkles radiating from the anchor points
- Micro-scratches catching the specular
- Faint grime along the lower edge, cleaner along the top
- Die-cut edges with very slight micro-serration
- A small nick, 3 mm, on the lower edge near frame left
- Static-clung dust particles on the face

**Print**
- Flexographic black ink sitting *on* the surface with slight physical relief
- Bold condensed grotesque capitals
- `PAID IN CASH · DO NOT CROSS` repeating along the length
- Ink density varies — some letters heavier, one or two thin enough to show the
  yellow through
- Tiny feathering at letter edges where ink spread into the plastic
- Letterforms compress and skew where the tape sags and twists
- At least one complete unbroken repetition legible near frame centre

**Motion**
- Caught mid-ripple in light wind, a shallow wave travelling its length
- Frozen sharp, no motion blur

---

## 5 · THE GROUND

Broom-finished concrete forecourt, wet from rain that stopped minutes ago.

**Texture**
- Fine parallel striations from the broom finish, running left to right
- Exposed aggregate where traffic has worn the surface
- Expansion joints every 3 m, filled with black sealant, cracked and shrunken
- A hairline crack running diagonally, filled with darker water

**Water**
- Not uniform. A thin film in the low spots, dry patches on the high points
- The film is what makes the light streak; the dry patches break the streaks up
- No deep puddles

**Wear**
- Tyre-polished lanes where vehicles run — smoother, darker, more reflective than
  the surrounding concrete
- One faint tyre track crossing the frame beyond the tape
- Round darker halos where fuel has dripped and soaked in over years
- A single thin iridescent oil film at the far edge of frame, muted — a faint
  bronze-and-violet shimmer, not a saturated rainbow

**Inside the cordon: bare concrete. No object of any kind.**

---

## 6 · THE AIR

Humid Caribbean evening. The air itself is slightly visible:

- Fine haze softening everything past 8 m
- Visible **bloom** around each canopy light — a soft halo from moisture in the air
- Suspended drizzle particles catching light near the lamps, only in the bright pools
- Warmer, denser haze low to the ground where evaporation is coming off the wet concrete

---

## 7 · BACKGROUND

Everything past the tape is dissolved. Present for light and shape only.

- **Canopy lights:** 5–7 warm orbs, varied in size and intensity, upper third of frame
- **Fuel pumps:** dark vertical silhouettes, no readable form, no branding
- **Price sign:** a soft warm rectangle far back, digits illegible
- **Canopy soffit edge:** a faint horizontal line above the light pools
- **Reflections:** vertical streaks of canopy light stretched down the wet ground,
  the brightest thing in the lower half of the frame, broken by the concrete texture

No people. No vehicles. No police vehicles or livery. No readable signage or
brand marks anywhere in frame.

---

## 8 · OPTICS & SENSOR

- **Bokeh:** circular from the rounded 11-blade aperture, going slightly cat's-eye
  toward the frame corners from mechanical vignetting
- **Longitudinal CA:** faint green fringing behind the focus plane, faint magenta
  in front — visible only at high-contrast edges
- **Vignetting:** 0.6 stop falloff at the corners, uncorrected
- **Highlight rolloff:** soft shoulder on the canopy lights, cores blooming to near
  white without hard clipping
- **Grain:** fine luminance grain, ISO 400 character, minimal chroma noise
- **Micro-contrast:** high on the tape print, falling away rapidly with distance

## 12 · REJECT

- Tape perfectly straight, flat or taut — must sag, twist and ripple
- Uniform ink density across the print
- Any object inside the cordon
- People, vehicles, police livery, readable brand names
- Daylight or full darkness — blue hour only
- Deep depth of field
- Saturated rainbow oil slick
- Uniform sheet of water rather than film-and-dry-patch
- HDR look, crushed blacks, clipped lamp cores
- Yellow pushed toward orange
- Sharp detail or bright highlight in the upper-left or lower-right zones
