# EVIDENCE BAG — CAROUSEL PAYOFF
## PRODUCTION SPECIFICATION · STILL PHOTOGRAPH
**4:5 VERTICAL | 1080 × 1350 DELIVERY | GENERATE AT 2160 × 2700**

Companion plate to `ig-evidence-bag.prompt.txt`. Same format as
`ig-cash-paid-in-cash.md`, with one section added for the label, because the
label is the only part of this image that must come back **empty**.

---

## 0 · WHY THIS IMAGE EXISTS

Slide 1 cordons off a cash purchase nobody recorded. This is the answer to it,
and the answer is not that somebody was caught — there was never a crime, only
a missing record. So the closing image is the same world with the cordon gone
and the transaction turned into an object: sealed, labelled, accounted for.

An evidence label is a form — item, date, custody, reference. That is a journal
entry in another uniform. The parallel is the whole idea, and it only lands if
the panel is blank enough for us to set a real posting into it.

---

## 1 · SUBJECT

A clear polyethylene evidence bag lying flat on wet concrete at dusk, sealed
along its top edge, with a **blank white write-on panel** across its lower half.

Inside: folded banknotes, creased, generic, no denomination or portrait legible.

The bag is the only sharp object in the photograph.

---

## 2 · FRAME

**Camera height:** 1.1 m, looking down. High enough that the bag reads as lying
on the ground rather than propped toward the lens.

**Camera tilt:** approximately 15° off vertical. Dead overhead reads as a
product mockup; a steep angle skews the panel so far that type set into it later
needs a perspective transform to sit believably. Fifteen degrees keeps the panel
close to rectangular and still looks photographed.

**Distance:** 0.6 m. Focus plane locked on the panel.

**Lens:** 85 mm prime, 11-blade rounded aperture.

**Aperture:** f/2.2. The panel is sharp; the concrete dissolves within a few
centimetres of it.

**Shutter:** 1/160 s. **ISO:** 400. **Sensor:** full-frame, 45 MP class.

---

## 3 · LIGHT

**Sky — ambient fill.** Deep indigo dusk, around 11,000 K, soft and sourceless.
Fills the shadows so nothing crushes to black.

**Practical key.** 4000 K, above and behind the bag at a low raking angle. This
is the important one: raking light is what makes the wrinkles in the plastic
read as plastic. Flat frontal light turns the bag into a grey rectangle.

- broken specular line along each crease
- a soft sheen across the sealed strip
- the panel stays evenly lit and free of hotspots, because type goes there

**Ground bounce.** Wet concrete returns warm light into the underside of the
turned-up corner.

**Ratio:** roughly 3:1 warm practical to cool ambient — the same 7,000 K split
that structures the slide 1 plate.

**No flash, no light source in frame, no hotspot on the panel.**

---

## 4 · THE BAG

0.06 mm polyethylene, slightly cloudy rather than optically clear.

**Geometry**
- Lies flat, filling the middle of the frame
- Rotated a few degrees off the frame's vertical, never square to it
- One corner turned up where it has not settled
- Slight pillowing where the notes sit inside

**Surface**
- Fine wrinkles across the whole face, catching the raking specular
- A tamper-evident seal strip along the top, adhesive texture visible, small
  air pockets trapped beneath it
- Faint fingerprints and water spots
- Die-cut edges with slight micro-serration
- Static-clung dust on the upper face

**Contents**
- Folded banknotes, creased and soft with handling
- **Generic. No denomination, no portrait, no serial, no country legible.**
  Rendered currency is rendered badly, and badly-rendered money reads as
  counterfeit — the last association a bookkeeping post can afford.
- Partially obscured by the cloudiness of the plastic

## 5 · THE LABEL — MUST COME BACK EMPTY

A white write-on panel bonded to the lower half of the bag.

**It carries nothing.** No printed fields, no ruled lines, no handwriting, no
barcode, no logo, no case number, no watermark. Clean matte paper stock.

This is the single hardest instruction in the spec and the one most likely to be
ignored, because every reference photograph of an evidence bag on earth has a
printed form on it. If the panel comes back with any marking, the plate is
unusable — we would be compositing over the model's output and it will show
through at the edges.

**Physical character it should have:**
- Matte, non-reflective, slightly toothy paper
- Edges very slightly lifted from the plastic
- One corner darkened where water has wicked into the stock
- A soft crease running through it

**Occupies roughly 45% of the bag's face**, low and centred, its long edge
close to parallel with the bag's own.

---

## 6 · THE GROUND

Broom-finished concrete, wet from rain that stopped minutes ago. Identical
treatment to the slide 1 plate.

- Fine parallel striations from the broom finish
- Thin water film in the low spots, dry patches on the high points
- An expansion joint with black sealant, cracked and shrunken
- A hairline crack filled with darker water
- Round darker halos where fuel has dripped and soaked in
- One muted bronze-and-violet oil sheen at the far edge — faint, never a
  saturated rainbow

---

## 7 · THE AIR

Humid Caribbean evening. Fine haze past 3 m. Warmer, denser haze low to the
ground where evaporation is coming off the wet concrete.

---

## 8 · BACKGROUND

Everything past the bag is dissolved — present for light and shape only.

- Two or three warm bokeh orbs from distant lamps, upper corners
- No canopy, no pumps, no tape, no vehicles, no people, no hands
- No readable signage or brand marks anywhere

The tape is deliberately absent. Its absence is the argument.

---

## 9 · OPTICS & SENSOR

- **Bokeh:** circular, going slightly cat's-eye toward the corners
- **Longitudinal CA:** faint green fringing behind the focus plane, magenta in
  front, only at high-contrast edges
- **Vignetting:** 0.6 stop falloff at the corners, uncorrected
- **Highlight rolloff:** soft shoulder on the speculars, no hard clipping
- **Grain:** fine luminance grain, ISO 400 character
- **Micro-contrast:** high on the plastic wrinkles, falling away with distance

## 12 · REFERENCE IMAGE

**Use `ref-forecourt-dusk.png`, NOT the full slide 1 plate.**

That file is the lower-left forecourt of the dusk plate, cut below the tape and
trimmed past the corner where the tape dips: 1900 × 1444 of wet concrete,
expansion joint, water film, amber lamp reflections, dusk grain and
depth-of-field falloff. Nothing else.

The reason is that a text instruction cannot out-argue a picture. Hand a model a
frame with barrier tape running across the middle and the tape IS the reference,
whatever the words say — you will spend three generations fighting it back out,
and §8 needs the tape gone because its absence is the argument. Cropping does in
pixels what the sentence was trying to do in prose.

Chromatic detection will not find the tape for you, incidentally: the wet ground
reflections are saturated amber and pass every yellow test the tape does. The
crop above was made geometrically — the tape runs down to the right, so cut
below its lowest point and drop the right margin.

Wording to pair with it:

> Match the light, colour temperature, wet concrete texture, film grain and
> depth-of-field character of the reference. Do not reproduce its composition.

The continuity we need is optical, not compositional — the bookend works only if
it reads as the same photographer at the same hour, not the same picture.

Creato Display. Never generated. Set into the blank panel in code, so the

AMOUNT       JMD 11,000

PAID         Cash from till
POSTED BY    CaribBooks
REF          DR Misc. Expense / CR Cash on Hand

Labels in Regular at roughly 60% of the value size, values in Bold, both in

same 12-column grid as every other slide.

This is the transaction from the WhatsApp thread, verbatim. The point of the
image is that the thing which did not exist on slide 1 now exists as an object,
so the figures have to be the real ones.

## 14 · REJECT

- **Any marking at all on the white panel** — the failure that makes the plate unusable
- Readable currency: denominations, portraits, serials, country names
- Barrier tape anywhere in frame
- People, hands, gloves, vehicles, branding
- The bag held, propped, or standing rather than lying flat
- Dead overhead or steeply angled — both break type set into the panel
- A hotspot or specular blowout on the panel
- Flat frontal light: the wrinkles must read
- Deep depth of field
- Saturated rainbow oil slick
- HDR look, crushed blacks, clipped speculars
- Daylight or full darkness — dusk only
